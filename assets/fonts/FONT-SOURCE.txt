Design choice: Comic Neue has a friendly handwriting character without joined letterforms. Use regular 400 for narrative text and bold 700 for short dialogue emphasis. This is a visual choice; no special reading or dyslexia claim is made. Use 1.2–1.3 line height, avoid italic, and keep each text step short.
